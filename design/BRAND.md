# Tundra identity

**Tundra** — Your bitcoin. Your hardware.

A restrained identity pass, not a redesign.

The ridge-and-strata mark is an original geometric prototype mark. It appears in the existing wallet-header icon, desktop wordmark, Settings and page favicon. No extra toolbar was introduced.

Dark neutrals are cool graphite; light neutrals are cool off-white. Bitcoin orange remains the primary action color. All palette values are in tokens.css; the identity layer is in brand.css.

Wallet, Activity, Coins, Receive, Send and Sign remain ordinary product terms. No snow, ice animation, themed labels or new financial capability was added.

Brand assets are supplied as SVG source in brand/. This delivery does not include fonts or a trademark-availability determination.
